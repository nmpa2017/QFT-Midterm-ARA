# Related Work: Midterm QFT Project

## Citation Footprint
- **Peskin & Schroeder, *An Introduction to Quantum Field Theory***: Primary source for Feynman rules, path integral formalism, and perturbative expansion techniques ($\lambda\phi^4$).
- **Zee, *Quantum Field Theory in a Nutshell***: Conceptual foundation for renormalization group flow and fixed points.
- **Weinberg, *The Quantum Theory of Fields***: Formal basis for S-matrix theory and causality.
- **FeynCalc/FeynArts Documentation**: Technical reference for implementing the numerical and symbolic diagrammatic expansions.
