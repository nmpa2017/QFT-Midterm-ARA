# Concepts: Midterm QFT Project

- **Generating Functional**: A functional $W[j]$ defined as a path integral over field configurations $\phi$, used to generate correlation functions of the field theory.
- **Feynman Propagator**: The Green's function for the Klein-Gordon operator, $\Delta_F(x-y) = \langle 0 | T\{\phi(x)\phi(y)\} | 0 \rangle$.
- **Wick's Theorem**: A theorem that transforms time-ordered products of field operators into sums of normal-ordered products of contractions.
- **Vertex Factor**: The momentum-space expression resulting from the interaction term in the Lagrangian (e.g., $-i\lambda$ for $\lambda\phi^4$).
- **Connected Correlation Function**: The subset of correlation functions that cannot be partitioned into separate, non-linked clusters.
