# Constraints: Midterm QFT Project

## Theoretical Limitations
- **Perturbativity**: The entire derivation is strictly limited to perturbative scalar theory; non-perturbative phenomena (such as solitonic solutions, confinement, or phase transitions in strong coupling) are excluded.
- **Dimensionality**: Fixed $d=4$.
- **Regularization**: UV divergences are addressed via standard $i\epsilon$ prescriptions for causal structure, but full renormalization (counterterms) is outside the scope of the midterm project (though recognized as a necessary next step).

## Scope
- Focus is on Scalar Field Theory ($\lambda\phi^4$). Spinor (Dirac) or Gauge (Vector) fields are not addressed.
