# Environment: Midterm QFT Project

## Dependencies
- Analytical framework: Pen and paper derivation, verified against standard textbook results (Peskin & Schroeder).
- Numerical verification (optional): Python with `numpy` and `sympy` for symbolic expansion of diagrammatic symmetry factors.

## Reproducibility
- The derivations are analytic. Results are reproducible by following the steps outlined in `/logic/experiments.md` using any standard QFT textbook.
